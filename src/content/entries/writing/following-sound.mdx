Following Sound traces the movement of music through physical space, community practice, and personal encounter. It is less about finished recordings and more about the conditions that make sound meaningful -- the rooms, the instruments, the people gathered, and the silences between.

This essay sits at the intersection of the music and field-work writing lanes.
