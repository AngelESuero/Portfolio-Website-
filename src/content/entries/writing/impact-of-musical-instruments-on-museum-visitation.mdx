This research paper investigates the relationship between musical instruments in museum environments and patterns of community visitation. The question is not only whether instruments attract visitors, but whether they change the nature of the visit itself -- turning observation into participation, and spectatorship into presence.

Connected to the broader AV/museum work thread and the writing on sound as a community practice.
